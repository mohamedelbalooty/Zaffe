## 2. Where Business Models Are NOT Directly Comparable

Direct feature checklists can mislead if they treat software capabilities and human agency labor as equivalent:

1. **Managed Agency & Staffing vs. Pure Self-Service SaaS:**
   - Hayhom sells **manual labor** bundled with software: graphic designers (`2–3` days per draft), call-center agents calling reluctant relatives on waiting lists (`Special`), private WhatsApp VIP support groups, and uniformed physical hall ushers with entry tables (`785–2,250 SAR` per 5-hour shift in `10` KSA + `4` GCC cities).
   - Zaffe is a **scalable self-service SaaS platform** targeting **Saudi Arabia (First)** and **Egypt (Second)** at **$0 marginal operational cost in Release 1** (`100% Free`). Zaffe should **NOT** copy uniformed staffing or outbound call centers in Release 1; instead, software-automate **90% of the coordination value**.
2. **Per-Invitee Telecom Arbitrage vs. Organic Viral Link Sharing:**
   - Hayhom charges `185–10,950 SAR` largely to subsidize commercial **WhatsApp Business API / SMS telecom fees**, replacement pools (`15%–50%`), and human labor.
   - Zaffe empowers the couple and their families to share **personalized links (`?g=inviteToken`) directly via their own WhatsApp** at **$0 telecom cost**, yielding higher open/reply trust from relatives and preserving `100%` free entry across KSA & Egypt.
