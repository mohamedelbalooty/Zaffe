## 2. Where Business Models Are NOT Directly Comparable

Direct feature checklists can mislead if they treat software capabilities and human agency labor as equivalent:

1. **Managed Agency & Staffing vs. Pure Self-Service SaaS:**
   - Hayhom sells **manual labor** bundled with software: graphic designers (`2–3` days per draft), call-center agents calling reluctant relatives on waiting lists (`Special`), private WhatsApp VIP support groups, and uniformed physical hall ushers with entry tables (`785–2,250 SAR` per 5-hour shift in `10` KSA + `4` GCC cities).
   - Zaffe is a **scalable self-service SaaS platform** targeting both **Saudi Arabia and Egypt** at **$0 marginal operational cost in Release 1** (`100% Free`). Zaffe should **NOT** copy uniformed staffing or outbound call centers in Release 1; instead, software-automate **90% of the coordination value** (personalized WA reminders, self-service guest list & family/children limits, zero-app browser QR scanning for family/ushers).
2. **Per-Invitee Telecom Arbitrage vs. Organic Viral Link Sharing:**
   - Hayhom charges `185–10,950 SAR` largely to subsidize commercial **WhatsApp Business API / SMS telecom fees**, replacement pools (`15%–50%`), and human labor.
   - Zaffe empowers the couple and their families to share **personalized links (`?g=token`) directly via their own WhatsApp** at **$0 telecom cost**, yielding higher trust from guests (messages come from a known relative/host number rather than an unfamiliar commercial sender!) and preserving `100%` free entry across Egypt & KSA.

## 4. Summary of Top Market Gaps Neither Competitor Solves Well Today

1. **Interactive Wedding Microsite + Real Venue QR Control in ONE Free Zero-App Flow:** Hayhom forces static graphic cards + mandatory app install + `185+ SAR` paywall + `72h` edit lock; Zaffe currently has the interactive microsite without guest lists or QR door check-in. Unifying both makes Zaffe uniquely superior in KSA & Egypt.
2. **Instant Self-Service Bilingual Family Privacy:** Keeping men's vs. women's lists or family sides clean, supporting optional bride name (`kunya`), and issuing scoped mobile web gate links without requiring family elders or hall ushers to install an app or verify App Store accounts.
3. **Affordable Cross-Market Luxury for Egypt + Saudi Arabia:** Serving Cairo/Alexandria with `EGP` awareness + classic/modern styling alongside Riyadh/Jeddah/Khobar with formal Arabic, Hijri accuracy, and private single-use QR entry control.
