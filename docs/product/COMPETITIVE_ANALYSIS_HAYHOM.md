## 3. Deep Capability Breakdown (`A–H`)

### A. Invitation Modes (`VERIFIED`)
Across all packages, hosts choose one of four structural modes after selecting a package:
1. **01:** Invitation with Acceptance, Apology, and Entry Code (`RSVP + QR`)
2. **02:** Invitation with Acceptance and Apology Only (`RSVP only`)
3. **03:** Invitation with Entry Code Only (`QR only`)
4. **04:** Invitation without Acceptance, Apology, or Entry Code (`Informational only`)

### B. Invitation Delivery & Automated Lifecycle (`VERIFIED` + `PARTIALLY VERIFIED`)
- Invitations are delivered via **WhatsApp or SMS** (`Self` / `Self Plus`: via Hayhom app system; `Special` / `Special Plus`: managed by Hayhom team) to **local and international numbers** across `36` country codes selectable on web trial form without international fee surcharge.
- Public homepage features a **"Try our invitation"** sandbox guarded by OTP verification before firing a single WhatsApp/SMS sample.
- Automated workflows claimed in FAQ: **1-day pre-event automatic reminder** to guests; **automatic venue/date update notification** on modifications; **Thanks message for attendees** reserved for `Special Plus`.
- *Limitation:* Exact WhatsApp BSP / SMS gateway vendor & template rejection failure rates behind the authenticated app are `UNKNOWN`.

### C. Guest & Children Management (`VERIFIED`)
- Dashboard tracks invitee status by name and phone (`Accepted`, `Declined`, `Pending / Did not respond`).
- **Replacement Invitations (`Compensation Balance`):** Declines or deactivated mis-sent invitations free up replacement slots up to `15%–50%` of package quota depending on tier.
- **Children's Invitations:** Separate invitation card for children + dedicated single-use **Children QR Code**.
- Guest responses happen on a mobile web page via single click — **guests do not download an app**.

### D. Event Access Control & QR Scanning (`VERIFIED`)
- Each invitee receives an individual **single-use QR passcode** (scanned once; duplicates rejected).
- Door scanning requires **no proprietary scanner hardware** — any designated mobile phone with the Hayhom app scans via camera and shows **real-time checked-in headcount vs. remaining invitees**.
- Available both **via self-service software** (`Self`+ mobile app) and **via staffed uniforms** (`Gatekeepers` add-on / `Special Plus`).

### E. Collaboration & Role-Based Privacy Isolation (`VERIFIED`)
- **Additional Inviter (`داعي إضافي`):** Appointed via phone contact picker; must accept invite inside Hayhom app; receives a **delegated quota** from the Main Inviter. **Strict bilateral isolation (verified in Terms & FAQ):** The Additional Inviter sees ONLY the guests they invited; **the Main Inviter CANNOT view the Additional Inviter's individual guest list** (only aggregate statistics!).
- **Gatekeeper / Portal Supervisor (`مشرف بوابة`):** Appointed via phone contact picker; can ONLY scan QR codes for approved invitees and see checked-in vs. remaining totals — no financial/admin access.

### F. Friction, Operational Policies & App User Sentiment (`VERIFIED`)
- **App-Mandatory Onboarding:** Hosts must download iOS (`42.9 MB`, iOS `15.0+`, v`3.3` on `4 Oct`) or Android (`5K+ Downloads`, updated `2026-11-09`) app and verify SMS OTP. Website provides static marketing, package selection, and help PDFs/videos (`7` PDFs + `1` MP4 needed to explain sending, delegating, scanning, and designing).
- **72-Hour Modification / Deletion Lockout:** Modifying or deleting an event is blocked `<72 hours` before the event start (`FAQ -> Event Details #1, #3`).
- **Cancellation & Postponement Penalties (`Cancellation Policy`):** `10%` fee before sending; `50%` fee after sending; **zero refund** `<1 week` before event; **30%–50% fee** to postpone an event after invitations were sent.
- **Compliance & Privacy (`Privacy Policy`, updated `2026-09-07`):** Explicitly structured around Saudi **PDPL (SDAIA)** with an online OTP-verified **Account Deletion Request** (`30-day` retention before purge) and `5-business-day` complaint SLA. Explicitly bans selling/using invitee numbers for marketing ads.

  - *Apple App Store (SA):* `4.3 / 5 (57 ratings)`. Positive themes (`2020–2023`): helpful staff (`Sara abdullrahman`, `LamaAlsaikhan`, `K.youssef`), ease of organizing events. Negative themes (`11/05/2025`): `Alanoudkhalid-1` (`"سئ جدااااا للاسف / التطبيق سيئ نصيحة لااحد يتعامل معاهم"`). Egypt App Store shows *"not enough ratings or reviews to display an overview"*.
  - *Google Play (SA / AR):* `4.5 / 5 (42 reviews)`, `5K+ downloads`. Positive: customer service & fair pricing (`Dr: Najla Abdulrahman`, `2020`), new concept. Negative technical friction: `مستخدم Google` (`4 Jan 2020`, `16 helpful votes`): `"لم يفتح التطبيق"` (app failed to open). *Note: small sample of public reviews; indicates app reliability & customer experience gaps but is not statistical proof of all users.*
