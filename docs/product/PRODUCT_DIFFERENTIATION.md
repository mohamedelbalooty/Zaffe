## 2. Recommended Positioning Statement

> **For couples and families planning weddings in Saudi Arabia and Egypt**, **Zaffe (زفّــة)** is the **zero-app, instant digital wedding experience and smart guest entry platform** that pairs **interactive luxury Arabic microsites** (with Umm al-Qura Hijri dates, zaffa audio, venue maps, and live timelines) with **personalized guest lists, single-use QR entry passes, and instant mobile-browser hall check-in** — giving couples complete control from design to arrival **without mandatory app downloads, punitive modification locks, or per-guest paywalls**.

## 3. Concrete Differentiation Pillars (Evidence-Backed vs. Hayhom)

### Pillar 1: Zero-App Friction for Everyone (Couple + Guest + Door Usher)
- **Hayhom (`VERIFIED`):** Forces hosts, additional inviters, and gatekeepers to install iOS/Android apps (`42.9 MB`), pass SMS OTP, and navigate `7` PDF tutorials + video (`how-to-use-app`); public Google Play feedback includes `"لم يفتح التطبيق"` (`16` helpful votes).
- **Zaffe Advantage:** Mobile web first across creation, customization, RSVP, personal QR pass saving, and browser camera/code door check-in — **0 MB app install** across iOS, Android, and desktop.

### Pillar 2: Total Host Agility — Zero `72-Hour` Lockout & Zero Postponement Penalties
- **Hayhom (`VERIFIED`):** Blocks event modification and deletion within `<72 hours` of the event (`FAQ`); deducts `10%–100%` on cancellations and `30%–50%` on post-send postponements.
- **Zaffe Advantage:** Unlimited real-time edits to hall location, map link, program timeline, countdown, and guest list right up to and during the wedding night at zero penalty.

### Pillar 3: Interactive Microsite Experience vs. Flat Static/MP4 Cards
- **Hayhom (`VERIFIED`):** Centers on single graphic card images (`75–255 SAR`) or animated/3D videos (`365–1,450 SAR`) with Accept/Decline buttons; locks **bilingual AR/EN** behind the `850+ SAR` `Special` tier.
- **Zaffe Advantage:** Every invitation is a fast, bespoke multi-section mobile experience — `6` luxury RTL themes, custom colors & typography, Umm al-Qura Hijri + configurable numerals (`F13`), interactive audio zaffa curtain, Google Maps card, timeline, dress code, and bilingual AR/EN support included free.

### Pillar 4: Personal WhatsApp Trust Without Telecom Tax
- **Hayhom (`VERIFIED`):** Charges `185–10,950 SAR` for app/system dispatch plus replacement quota caps (`15%–50%`).
- **Zaffe Advantage (`F16` Target):** Generates **personalized named links (`?g=token`)** with 1-click pre-filled Arabic/English WhatsApp launch directly from the host's or mother/sister's trusted WhatsApp number — **higher open & reply trust from family guests, zero telecom markup, and zero artificial cap on declines**.

### Pillar 5: Smart QR Access Control Accessible to Both KSA Hotels and Every Egyptian Venue
- **Hayhom (`VERIFIED`):** Staffed gatekeepers cost `785–2,250 SAR` and operate ONLY in `10` Saudi cities + `4` Gulf capitals — **zero presence in Egypt**. Even self-scanning requires downloading Hayhom's native app.
- **Zaffe Advantage (`F16` Target):** Issues **single-use pure-SVG QR passes** (opaque cryptographic tokens with zero PII inside the QR string) paired with **scoped browser Gatekeeper links (`/gate/{token}`)** supporting both live camera scanning and instant code/name verification on any reception smartphone — working identically in Riyadh, Al-Ahsa, Hail, Cairo, Alexandria, or Upper Egypt for free.

### Pillar 6: Privacy-By-Design for Conservative Gulf & Egyptian Families
- **Zaffe Advantage:** Optional photo-free layouts, planned optional `bride_name` (`kunya`/family naming per `SAUDI_MARKET_DECISIONS.md`), opaque QR tokens free of exposed phone numbers/emails, isolated check-in links requiring no owner credentials, and OWASP-sanitized CSV rosters.
